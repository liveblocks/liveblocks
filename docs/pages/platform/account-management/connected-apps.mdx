_Connected apps_ are AI apps, such as Claude, Cursor, or Codex, that you’ve
connected to a Liveblocks project by signing in through the
[MCP server](/docs/tools/mcp-server). Each connection can access one project,
with read-only or read and write access.

## See and disconnect your apps

Any user can see and disconnect the apps they’ve connected.

1. Go to
   [**Personal settings → Connected apps**](https://liveblocks.io/dashboard/settings/connected-apps)
   in the dashboard.
2. Find the app, then click **Disconnect…** and confirm.

The app loses access within about two minutes. To use it again, connect it again
from the app.

## See and disconnect your team’s apps

Team owners can see every app their team’s members have connected to the team’s
projects, including who connected each one.

1. In the dashboard, open your team, then go to **Settings → Connected apps**.
2. Find the app, then click **Disconnect…** and confirm.

Apps connected by someone who has left the team stop working within ten minutes,
and are labelled as connected by a former member.
