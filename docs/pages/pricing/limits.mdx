### What happens when you reach a limit

On Free, limits are hard caps. Liveblocks emails your account owners and shows a
dashboard banner as you approach one, then pauses that activity until it resets
at the start of the next month. Upgrade to [Pro](/dashboard/billing?plan=pro) or
[Team](/dashboard/billing?plan=team) to lift the cap.

On Pro and Team, metered usage above your monthly credits keeps running and is
billed as overage. Starting November 12, 2026, reaching your monthly active room
allowance blocks the creation of new rooms, as described below.

## Monthly active room limits [#monthly-active-room-limits]

Starting **November 12, 2026**, Pro workspaces have an allowance of
{/* prettier-ignore */} <Limits.ProMaxMonthlyActiveRooms /> monthly active rooms, and Team workspaces have
{/* prettier-ignore */} <Limits.TeamMaxMonthlyActiveRooms />. These limits apply to the January 2026 and April
2026 Pro and Team plans, on both monthly and annual billing.

The allowance is shared across all projects and regions in a workspace. Each
distinct room counts once during the month, using the existing
[monthly active room definition](/docs/pricing/faqs#What-is-a-monthly-active-room).
Free plan limits, Enterprise and custom agreements, and monthly active user
limits are unchanged.

### Reaching the allowance

Once a workspace reaches its monthly active room allowance, creating new rooms
is blocked. All existing rooms remain usable, including rooms that have not yet
been active in the current month. Those rooms still count when they become
active, so monthly active room usage can exceed the allowance.

There is no grace period or pay-as-you-go option for creating new rooms after
reaching the allowance. To resume creating new rooms before the monthly reset,
upgrade to a plan with an allowance above your current monthly active room
usage. Pro workspaces can [upgrade to Team](/dashboard/billing?plan=team) if its
allowance is sufficient. If Team's allowance is insufficient, whether you are
currently on Pro or Team,
[contact sales about an Enterprise allowance](/contact/sales).

Email alerts are sent at 50%, 80%, and 100% of the allowance, once per threshold
per workspace per month. If usage crosses several thresholds at once, only the
highest applicable alert is sent.

### Monthly reset and the first allowance

The allowance resets on the first day of each calendar month at 00:00 UTC,
including for annual plans.

The first allowance covers **November 12–30, 2026**. Workspaces receive the full
monthly allowance, counting only activity after enforcement is activated on
November 12. Activity before activation does not count toward this first
allowance. The next reset is **December 1, 2026, at 00:00 UTC**, followed by the
regular UTC calendar months.

Note that when one realtime data structure is nested inside another, it does not
count towards the limit. Only the JSON leaves of your data structure count
towards the limit. For example, if a `LiveList` is nested inside a `LiveObject`,
the `LiveList` and its contents do not count towards the `LiveObject`'s data
limit.

For large files such as images and videos, use `LiveFile` instead of storing
binary data or base64 strings in realtime data structures.
