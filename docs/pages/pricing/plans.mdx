Liveblocks provides the realtime infrastructure to power collaborative
experiences for human and agentic users.

Pricing is usage-based, metered on the infrastructure your app actually uses,
like collaboration time, storage, comments, and notifications. Pro and Team
include a monthly credit allowance that your usage draws from, so your cost
aligns with how your app uses Liveblocks. The [Free plan](#Free-plan) includes
fixed monthly usage allowances instead.

Credits are denominated in US dollars, cover your
[metered usage](#Metered-usage) first, and reset at the start of each billing
cycle. Team offers several
[credit packages](/docs/pricing/billing#Team-credit-packages) that include more
credit value than they cost, and annual billing gives you two months free. See
[Billing](/docs/pricing/billing) for details, or [contact sales](/contact/sales)
for Enterprise pricing.

## Metered usage

Metered usage is billed per unit, only for what you actually use. Your monthly
credits cover this usage first, so you only pay for anything above them. The
same rates apply on Pro and Team; Enterprise contracts may include volume
discounts or custom allocations.

## Free plan

The Free plan is for personal development, prototyping, and testing. It includes
the full platform at no cost and requires no credit card. It doesn't use
credits: instead, each metered unit has a fixed monthly allowance. The "Powered
by Liveblocks" badge is displayed in your application, and usage is limited to
non-commercial, personal projects per the
[fair use guidelines](/docs/pricing/faqs#Commercial-usage).

|                      | Free                                            |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Description**      | Personal development, prototyping, and testing. |
| **Base price**       | $0                                              |
| **Included credits** | [Monthly allowances](#Free-monthly-allowances)  |
