Liveblocks provides the realtime infrastructure to power collaborative
experiences for human and agentic users.

Pricing is usage-based, metered on the infrastructure your app actually uses,
like collaboration time, storage, comments, and notifications. Pro and Team
include a monthly credit allowance that your usage draws from, so your cost
aligns with how your app uses Liveblocks. Free includes fixed monthly usage
allowances instead.

Four plans scale from prototype to production: Free, Pro, Team, and Enterprise.

## Credits and Free allowances

Pro and Team include a monthly credit allowance that covers your metered usage
first. Credits are denominated in US dollars and reset at the start of each
billing cycle. Usage above your credits is billed at the
[metered rates](#Metered-usage) below. Team offers several
[credit packages](/docs/pricing/billing#Team-credit-packages) that include more
credit value than they cost. Enterprise plans include a custom credit allowance
negotiated for your workload.

The Free plan doesn't use credits. Instead, it includes fixed monthly usage
allowances for each metered unit.

If your account exceeds an allowance, the affected feature pauses until

pay for anything above them. On Free, each metered unit has a fixed

include volume discounts or custom allocations.

### Free

The Free plan is for personal development, prototyping, and testing. It includes
the full platform at no cost, with fixed
[monthly usage allowances](#Free-monthly-allowances) for each metered unit
instead of credits.

- No credit card required.
- Up to <Limits.FreeMaxMonthlyActiveRooms /> monthly active rooms and
  <Limits.FreeTeamMembersPerAccount /> dashboard seats.
- The "Powered by Liveblocks" badge is displayed in your application.
- Limited to non-commercial, personal projects. See the
  [fair use guidelines](/docs/pricing/limits/fair-use-guidelines#Commercial-usage).

### Pro

The Pro plan is for developers shipping collaborative features. It includes
everything in Free, plus:

- <Limits.ProMonthlyCredits /> of monthly credits for
  <Limits.ProMonthlyPrice /> per month, or
  <Limits.ProAnnualPricePerMonth /> per month billed annually.
- Usage beyond your credits continues at the [metered rates](#Metered-usage).
- Remove the "Powered by Liveblocks" badge.
- Up to <Limits.ProMaxMonthlyActiveRooms /> monthly active rooms and
  <Limits.ProTeamMembersPerAccount /> dashboard seats.

### Team

The Team plan is for teams shipping collaborative features at scale. It includes
everything in Pro, plus:

- A choice of [credit packages](/docs/pricing/billing#Team-credit-packages),
  starting at
  <Limits.TeamAnnualCredits tier={600} /> of monthly credits for
  <Limits.TeamMonthlyPriceForTier tier={600} /> per month.
- <Limits.TeamTeamMembersPerAccount /> dashboard seats included, then
  <Limits.DashboardSeatsOveragePrice />.
- [SAML Single Sign-on (SSO)](/docs/platform/account-management/saml) and a
  private Slack channel.
- SOC 2 report, available with annual billing.
- HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) as a paid add-on at
  <Limits.HipaaBaaPricePerMonth /> per month, billed annually.

### Enterprise

The Enterprise plan is for organizations with premium security and partnership
needs. It includes everything in Team, plus:

- Custom credit allocations and volume discounts, with invoice billing.
- Multi-region hosting.
- [Management API](/docs/platform/management-api),
  [SCIM / directory sync](/docs/platform/account-management/directory-sync), and
  advanced permissions.
- Higher-touch support with a <Limits.EnterpriseUptimeSla /> uptime SLA.
- Self-hosting as a paid add-on: run Liveblocks Sync in your own cloud so
  collaboration data stays within your infrastructure, while Liveblocks provides
  ongoing releases, security updates, deployment guidance, and enterprise
  support. See [data storage](/docs/platform/data-storage#Self-hosting) for more
  information.

Enterprise limits are customized for your organization.
