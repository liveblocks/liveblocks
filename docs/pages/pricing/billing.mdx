Understand how your bill is calculated: monthly credits, Team credit packages,
overage, annual billing, invoices, and tracking your usage. For plan prices,
Free allowances, and metered rates, see [Plans](/docs/pricing/plans).

## Credits

Pro and Team include a monthly credit allowance that covers your
[metered usage](/docs/pricing/plans#Metered-usage) first. Credits are
denominated in US dollars. Billing runs monthly, from the 1st to the last day of
the month. Credits reset at the start of each billing cycle and do not roll
over.

The Free plan doesn't use credits. It includes fixed
[monthly usage allowances](/docs/pricing/plans#Free-monthly-allowances) for each
metered unit, which reset on the same monthly cycle.

Enterprise plans include a custom monthly credit allowance negotiated for your
workload. [Contact sales](/contact/sales) to discuss your requirements.

## Overage

Usage above your monthly credits is overage. On Pro and Team, there are no hard
caps for metered usage: usage beyond your credits keeps running and is billed at
the [metered rates](/docs/pricing/plans#Metered-usage) at the end of the month,
with email alerts along the way. Separate
[plan limits](/docs/pricing/plans#Plan-limits), including the monthly active
room allowance, still apply.

On Free, there is no overage. If your account exceeds an allowance, the affected
feature pauses until allowances reset at the start of the next calendar month or
you [upgrade your account](/dashboard/billing) to a paid plan. See
[what happens when you run out of credits](/docs/pricing/faqs#What-happens-when-I-run-out-of-Liveblocks-credits)
in the FAQs.

## January 2026 plans

January 2026 Pro and Team plans have no monthly active room cap. Additional
rooms continue to be billed under the existing plan, and overage spending alerts
continue as before. The monthly active room limits taking effect on November 12,
2026 apply to April 2026 Pro and Team plans, including monthly and annual
subscriptions. All November activity counts toward those allowances.

The January plans' monthly webhook event allowances are unchanged. See the
[monthly active room FAQ](/docs/pricing/faqs#What-happens-when-I-reach-the-monthly-active-room-limit)
for details about room limits.

## Annual billing

Pay annually to get two months free compared with monthly billing. Credits still
reset each month.

- Pro: <Limits.ProAnnualPricePerMonth /> per month billed annually, instead of
  <Limits.ProMonthlyPrice /> per month.
- Team: see the annual price for each [credit package](#Team-credit-packages)
  above.

On Team, the SOC 2 report is available with annual billing only.

## Invoices and payment

Paid plans are billed by card. Invoicing is available on Enterprise.
[Contact sales](/contact/sales) to discuss upgrading to an Enterprise plan.

You can view and download past invoices from your
[billing settings](/dashboard/billing).

## Tracking your usage

The [usage page](/dashboard/usage) in your dashboard shows real-time usage
broken down by metric, current credit consumption, and projected month-end
spend. It is accessible to all account Owners. Liveblocks also emails your
account owners as you approach your limits.
