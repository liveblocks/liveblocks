Liveblocks is a realtime collaboration platform that allows you to build
multiplayer applications where users and agents can work together. It provides a
sync engine that powers shared documents, text editing, presence, and more. Ably
is a realtime messaging service with Pub/Sub channels, presence, shared maps and
counters (Ably LiveObjects), a chat API, and a transport for streaming AI
responses.

## Which should you choose? [#which-should-you-choose]

Ably and Liveblocks solve different problems. Ably is how messages and state
move between clients at scale, whereas Liveblocks is how people edit artifacts
together.

Choose Liveblocks when several people work on the same document, board, or text
at once, and the product needs comments and notifications around that work, such
as a design review where teammates annotate a canvas and resolve threads. Choose
Ably when the job is delivering messages to many clients, such as a live score
feed, a chat room with thousands of viewers, or streaming an AI response to a
user's phone and laptop at the same time. Some products use Ably for high-volume
messaging and Liveblocks for the documents people edit together.

## Choose the interface and the infrastructure together [#choose-the-interface-and-the-infrastructure-together]

For a design-review feature, users annotate an object, mention a teammate,
resolve a thread, and come back through a notification. That workflow needs
shared canvas data, comments attached to the canvas, and notifications.

With [Liveblocks Comments](/docs/products/comments/default-components), you can
style the default components or combine hooks and primitives for a custom
interface, so you decide how much of the commenting UI to build yourself.
[Ably Chat](https://ably.com/docs/chat) is built for conversations in rooms and
ships a React UI Kit for that. Pick the one that matches the conversation in
your app: a discussion attached to a document, a group chat, or both.

## How does Liveblocks Storage compare with Ably LiveObjects? [#how-does-liveblocks-storage-compare-with-ably-liveobjects]

Ably LiveObjects is a much smaller toolkit. It has two types, a key-value
[`LiveMap`](https://ably.com/docs/liveobjects/map) and a
[`LiveCounter`](https://ably.com/docs/liveobjects/counter), and everything you
store is built from those. Note the name clash: Liveblocks also has a `LiveMap`,
and the two are unrelated. Liveblocks [Storage](/docs/products/sync/storage) has
five: `LiveObject`, `LiveMap`, `LiveList`, `LiveText`, and `LiveFile` for
uploaded files, and they nest to any depth with every level merging field by
field. Both keep shared state in sync across clients; the difference is how much
of your document they can represent without you writing merge logic.

For a design-review app, that plays out like this. Votes on a proposal are the
one case Ably LiveObjects handles more directly: an Ably `LiveCounter` sums
concurrent votes, whereas in Liveblocks you record each person's vote as its own
entry. An ordered agenda with editable paragraphs is a `LiveList` of
`LiveObject`s with `LiveText` inside; on Ably you would design the ordering
scheme and bolt on a text CRDT. A conversation attached to a shape is Comments
with the shape ID as metadata; on Ably it is a Chat room per shape or your own
message format.

Ably fits applications built around messages, counters, and flat shared state
delivered to many clients. Liveblocks fits applications built around structured
shared documents and the review workflows around them.

## How long is data kept? [#how-long-is-data-kept]

In Liveblocks, permanently. Storage and Yjs documents are
[stored until you delete them](/docs/platform/data-storage), with
[version history](/docs/products/sync/version-history) on top, so a proposal
nobody opens for two years is still there, unchanged, when someone does.

In Ably LiveObjects, until the data goes unchanged for the
[retention period](https://ably.com/docs/liveobjects/storage): configurable from
24 hours to 90 days, 90 days by default, and longer only by arrangement with
Ably. When it expires, the channel resets to an empty object. Ably's own
suggestion for a permanent record is to read the objects over its REST API and
store them in your database, which makes your database, not Ably, the system of
record for anything long-lived. That is workable for a leaderboard or a live
poll; it is a second storage layer to build and reconcile for documents people
come back to.

Reconnection is similar on both sides. Ably replays missed operations if the
client was away for under two minutes and resends the full object state
otherwise. Liveblocks clients resync the document on reconnect and send any
edits queued while offline in the session.

## How do they support AI features? [#how-do-they-support-ai-features]

Both do, for different jobs.
[Ably AI Transport](https://ably.com/docs/ai-transport) gives an agent
conversation a durable session: streamed responses, reconnection, and access
from several devices, optionally with Ably LiveObjects for shared session state.
Liveblocks lets [server-side code](/docs/products/sync/server-side-editing),
including agents, edit shared documents alongside users. A resumable agent chat
and an agent editing a shared document are different requirements, and an
application can need both.

## How do the billing units compare? [#how-do-the-billing-units-compare]

They don't map one-to-one. Ably's [Standard package](https://ably.com/pricing)
starts at $29 per month, with usage billed on top at $2.50 per million messages,
$1 per million connection minutes, and $1 per million channel minutes. For
[LiveObjects](https://ably.com/docs/liveobjects/pricing), every operation counts
as messages: a map set is one inbound message, and delivering that update counts
as one outbound message per connected client. Initial sync counts one outbound
message per object.

As an example, 100 map-set operations delivered to three connected clients
produce 100 inbound and 300 outbound messages. That excludes initial sync,
connection minutes, and channel minutes.

Liveblocks bills collaboration time, data updates, storage, and selected
collaboration features. Those units do not translate into Ably message counts,
so estimate both with the same editing session and feature scope. Your
[plan's credits](/docs/pricing/plans) apply once across all Liveblocks usage.

Ably is a managed service. Liveblocks offers
[self-hosting](/docs/platform/data-storage#Self-hosting) as a paid add-on on the
Enterprise plan. If you self-host, your own servers, database, backups, and
operations are part of the budget.
